# Reference notes: "Focus transition"

Source: https://www.pinterest.com/pin/646970302811929478/ (repin of an Instagram post by @mosyenboy, "focus: kinetic typography", fan-made). Pulled with treg on 2026-10-22. Used as a style reference only; no frames or audio from it go into our graphics.

Files: `focus-transition.mp4` (18s, 702x494, 30fps, has audio), `focus-transition-cover.jpg`.

## Look
- Off-white background (~#f2f2f2), black type, one accent: Mac-folder blue (~#4aa8ef) and text-selection blue (~#2f6bff handles, light blue box).
- Type collage: one word mixes fonts — italic serif, heavy grotesk, pixel/dithered letters, Korean glyphs.
- UI props: blue folders, selected-text box with round drag handles, typing cursor, photo thumbnails with black count badges, 3D keycap.
- Small 3D objects replace letters (tin can for "o", lemon inside `{ }`, chess knight).
- Black circle "bubble" letters that rearrange.

## Motion
- Hard, fast cuts (~0.3–0.6s per beat). Snappy ease-out, little overshoot.
- Letters scatter and reassemble; letters swap fonts mid-word.
- Type-on with blinking cursor.

## Transitions seen
1. **Ring spin:** words wrapped in circles, spinning and zooming toward camera.
2. **Block invert wipe:** chunky black/white blocks fill the frame.
3. **Folder scatter:** blue folders fly in from edges and across.
4. **Word slam:** one giant word, rotated, fills the frame.
5. **Selection grab:** a word gets selected (blue box + handles) and dragged.
